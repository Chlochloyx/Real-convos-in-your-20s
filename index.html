why this exists

Nobody really explains how to be an adult.

A lot of it comes down to being stuck in this rat race in our heads: comparing ourselves to everyone around us, feeling like we're never doing enough. Somewhere along the way we started believing that the more packed our schedules are, the more successful we must be, so we keep stacking things on until we burn out and neglect our own health in the process, and barely notice it happening. I used to think everyone else had this figured out. I don't think that anymore.

This site is just where I write that down: the ordinary version, not the tidy one. If you're 20-something and also unsure about most of it, you're probably not behind. Most people are just quieter about it, until they find somewhere to put it down. That's what the wall above is for, not just my part of it.
