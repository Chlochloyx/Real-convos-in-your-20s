Aug 8
growth

My biggest fear is looking back and realizing nothing changed

My biggest fear isn't even failing, it's looking back at myself in one year, three years, five years and realizing nothing actually changed, that I didn't really improve. It's good to have goals so we keep climbing, but the annoying thing about being human is we always strive for more, we're never just satisfied and staying there.

I really thought once I entered uni I'd finally feel happy and content. I got in, don't get me wrong it wasn't easy staying consistent throughout poly just to get here, but once I was here I just kept wanting more again. We always feel like we're never enough, that's just how it goes I think.

I think it's important to actually sit down and look back once in a while instead of only looking forward, and celebrate that, because that dream you always had, the one you don't even bring up anymore, you've probably already gone and achieved some version of it without even noticing.

Jul 30
family

Small habit, same one name every time

One small habit I've picked up that's actually been really great, every now and then I sit down and write out what I'm grateful for, doesn't matter where I am or what kind of week it's been. And honestly, most of the time it just comes down to one thing. My family.

No matter how far I am, how busy I get with work or school, whatever happens, they're always there. Doesn't matter how much I've messed up or how little I've checked in. And for that I'm genuinely grateful, more than I probably say out loud.

It's easy to get caught up in work and studies and forget the people around you. So this is just me reminding myself, don't forget to actually spend time with the people you love while you can. Cherish it.

Jul 10
new york

Do the thing that scares you, you'll never know what you'd have missed

Sometimes you just have to do the thing that scares you the most. You never know what you'll miss out on if you don't try, and I'd rather try than spend years regretting it. Of course there's so many unknowns, but you've just got to do what's best for you.

Going to New York for NOC was scary, I'd never been to the States before and this time it was alone, so far from home. Everyone around me was worried the whole time because of how it's portrayed in the media, like it's unsafe. And I'm not going to deny it, it can be unsafe, you do still have to be vigilant. But it's honestly one of the best decisions I've ever made.

Jun 20
career

What do you mean after 20 years of school, this is what you get

The economy is not the best right now and so many of my peers are struggling to even get a job. Sometimes at night I'll think, am I even in the right direction, should I have taken another degree, business is so general and so many people are struggling to get a job after grad, that worries me a lot.

Like what do you mean, after going through 20 years of education, this is what you get. I was crashing out too because I was trying to find an internship for summer, applied to so many, only got a few interviews, and then when I actually get into the interview round there's so many candidates to compete against. So stressful. It's just tough, makes me think how I'm even going to make a living in the future.
