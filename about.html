  • Five-year planWorking on it

    • "Some days I just feel lost, like I'm not doing enough, wondering if I'm even on the right path."

    I started this for a pretty ordinary reason. Some nights I just lie awake thinking about life and wondering if I'm doing enough, and it starts to consume me a bit. Time feels like it's moving so fast, like I blinked and I'm suddenly closer to my 30s than I expected, wondering where all the carefree years went. I'm realising there's more responsibility coming, parents getting older, actually having to start working life, and it's a lot of pressure some days. But when I talk to people around me, it turns out a lot of them feel the exact same way. That's really why this space exists, not just for me to get it out of my head, but so nobody has to feel like they're the only one going through it. I'm just a girl figuring life out same as everyone else here, Singaporean, based in Singapore, finishing up a business degree at NUS if the details matter to you.

    If I'm honest, a big part of it comes from being stuck in this rat race in my head: comparing myself to everyone around me, feeling like I'm never doing enough. Somewhere along the way I started believing that the more packed my schedule looked, the more successful I was, so I kept stacking things on top of each other until I burnt out and started neglecting my own health without even realising it was happening.

    Some of what's actually true right now: I still don't know what I'm doing after graduation, and I say that out loud less than I probably should. I compare myself to friends who seem to have it together, even though I know that's not the whole picture. A rejection email once threw off my entire afternoon more than I wanted to admit. None of this made it onto a highlight reel either, until I started writing it down here.

    There's also a lot I genuinely don't know at this stage, and sometimes that scares me more than I let on. I'm trying to remember that having the courage to step out of your comfort zone matters too, and that some of the best things that have happened to me were things I did scared anyway.

    If any of this sounds familiar, get in touch, or pin your own version on the wall.
