let's talk

Let's grab a matcha together, if you'd like.

This isn't a formal club or a programme. It's just an open door, for anyone who's 20-something and quietly figuring out career, travel, or life in general, and would rather not do it entirely alone.

things we could talk about

Any of this works.

Career confusion

Internship anxiety, choosing between "safe" and "interesting," or just not knowing what you want yet.

Travel questions

Solo trip nerves, exchange or internship-abroad decisions, or trip notes from Vietnam, New York, or China.

Post-grad uncertainty

What happens after school ends and the plan you were "supposed" to have doesn't exist yet.

Just need to vent

Sometimes you don't need advice, just someone to say "same" back. That's fine here too.

Matcha recommendations

I have opinions on ceremonial versus culinary grade and will share them without much prompting.

Whatever's on your mind

The thoughts that don't fit anywhere else. Those are usually the better conversations anyway.
